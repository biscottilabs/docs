# Arc Airdrop Questions and Biscotti Rewards

If you are researching an **Arc airdrop**, distinguish network announcements,
community programs, and rewards paid by individual applications. Biscotti
Finance is a DEX and token launchpad on Arc. Its trading, liquidity, and
reward features have their own documented rules.

This guide does not announce an Arc or BSCT airdrop or provide an eligibility
checker. Use the sources below to check the rules for a specific program.

## Does using Biscotti qualify me for an Arc airdrop?

Activity on Biscotti does not by itself establish eligibility for an Arc
airdrop. Swaps, liquidity deposits, token launches, and testnet transactions
are protocol interactions. Eligibility for a separate distribution must be
defined by that program's official announcement.

For the Arc Architects community program specifically, Arc's
[official tiers and benefits](https://community.arc.io/public/resources/architects-tiers-and-benefits)
state that points carry no monetary value and do not confer or guarantee
token, financial reward, or airdrop eligibility.

## Where should I check announcements?

| Topic | Official source |
| --- | --- |
| Arc network and ecosystem news | [Arc blog](https://www.arc.io/blog) |
| Arc wallet and network setup | [Arc documentation](https://docs.arc.io/arc/references/connect-to-arc) |
| Arc Architects program rules | [Arc House program terms](https://community.arc.io/public/resources/architects-terms-and-conditions) |
| Biscotti product and reward announcements | [@biscottidex on X](https://x.com/biscottidex) |
| Biscotti deployments and token plans | [Contract Addresses](/developers/addresses) and [Roadmap](/roadmap) |

A usable reward announcement should identify the network, reward asset,
eligibility rules, distribution dates, and the official claim process, if
one is required. Verify those details against the issuer's source before
using a claim link.

## Which rewards does Biscotti document?

| Mechanism | How it works | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liquidity provider fees | A liquidity position earns a share of applicable trading fees | [Concentrated liquidity](/concentrated-liquidity) |
| Launchpad creator fees | The creator receives 70% of trading fees through the selected fee mode | [Launchpad fees](/launchpad/fees) |
| Launchpad holder rewards | Tokens using this fee mode stream the creator's USDC fee share to eligible holders | [Holder rewards](/launchpad/fees#holder-rewards) |
| Farm rewards | Staked LP tokens or V3 positions earn BSCT under the farm's emission rules | [Farms](/farms) |
| Coffee Pool rewards | A funded campaign pays rewards to stakers during its reward period | [Coffee Pools](/coffee-pools) |
| Trading Battle prizes | Season allocations are claimed with a Merkle proof before the deadline | [Rewards and claims](/trading-battles/rewards) |

Check the network and campaign before participating. V3 and the launchpad
are deployed on mainnet; BSCT and farms are currently documented on testnet.
The [deployment reference](/developers/addresses) identifies the contracts
for each network.

## Are faucet tokens an airdrop?

Testnet faucet tokens let you test transactions on Arc Testnet. Receiving
them does not create a claim to mainnet tokens. The [testnet quick start](/quick-start)
explains how to use them with Biscotti.

## Is BSCT the Arc gas token?

No. Arc uses USDC for gas. [BSCT](/token) is Biscotti's protocol reward token.
Tokens created through the [launchpad](/launchpad) are separate assets with
their own contracts.
