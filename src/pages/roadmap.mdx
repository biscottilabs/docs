# Roadmap 🚧

**Detailed roadmap coming soon.**

The team is finalizing the public roadmap. High-level direction:

## Now: ARC Testnet

- ✅ Concentrated liquidity (full V3 suite)
- ✅ Stable pools (USDC/EURC live)
- ✅ Smart Router with split routing
- ✅ Farms (V3 + classic), Coffee Pools, SmartChef
- ✅ Trading Battles with on-chain Merkle claims
- ✅ Analytics subgraphs

## Live: Arc Mainnet

- ✅ Concentrated liquidity (V3), owned by Biscotti
- ✅ [Token launchpad](/launchpad)
- ✅ Analytics and launchpad subgraphs

## Next: hardening for mainnet

- Multisig + timelock over all admin functions
- LmPool tick-cross hook in a forked V3 core (fair in-range farm rewards)
- Security audits & public bug-bounty program

## Later: the rest of Biscotti on mainnet

- Stable pools, farms and BSCT on Arc Mainnet
  ([live addresses](/developers/addresses))
- BSCT buy-back-and-burn activation ([Tokenomics](/token#tokenomics))
- Governance over emission weights and burn parameters
- More stable pairs, more battle formats
