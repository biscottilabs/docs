# Tokens on Arc Mainnet

Arc supports stablecoins, tokenized assets, and tokens created by applications
and their users. **Biscotti Finance** provides a DEX and token launchpad for
trading and creating tokens on Arc Mainnet.

Use this guide to identify a token by its network and contract address,
understand its role, and find the appropriate trading or launch guide.

## Arc Mainnet token addresses

These are the assets recorded in Biscotti's
[mainnet deployment reference](/developers/addresses). They are a starting
list, not a complete directory of every token on Arc. An address in this
table does not mean that every pair has liquidity on Biscotti.

| Token | Role | Arc Mainnet contract address |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USDC | US dollar stablecoin and Arc's gas currency | `0x3600000000000000000000000000000000000000` |
| EURC | Euro stablecoin | `0xbEf5f6d51CB62b58e6A8f77868681825C6fe21c1` |

Arc's [mainnet launch announcement](https://www.arc.io/blog/arc-economic-os-internet)
describes the Circle assets available in the ecosystem. Asset-specific
transfer or access rules still apply.

## Check a token before importing it

A token symbol is not a unique identifier. Different contracts can use the
same name or symbol, and the same symbol can appear on several networks.

1. Confirm that your wallet is on Arc Mainnet, chain ID `5042`.
2. Copy the contract address from the issuer or the relevant deployment record.
3. Look up that address in [Arc Explorer](https://explorer.arc.io) and check
   the contract and token details.
4. Compare the address with the token selected in Biscotti before approving
   a transaction.

For tokens created through Biscotti, use the token's launch page and the
[launchpad contract reference](/launchpad/contracts) to identify its pool and
contracts. The [launchpad safety guide](/launchpad/safety) explains the
liquidity lock and the limits of that protection.

## How to trade Arc tokens

Connect to [Biscotti Finance](https://biscotti.finance), select Arc Mainnet,
and choose a pair in **Swap**. The quote depends on the liquidity available
for the route and the size of your trade.

Review the output amount, price impact, and slippage setting before confirming.
Keep some USDC available for gas. See the [Arc Mainnet wallet guide](/arc-mainnet)
for setup and the [swap guide](/swap) for how execution works.

## Create your own token on Arc

The [Biscotti launchpad](/launchpad) creates a fixed-supply token with a USDC
liquidity pool. All one billion tokens enter the launch pool, with no team
allocation or presale. The liquidity positions remain locked in the vault.

Creators choose between [Standard and Moon curves](/launchpad/curves) and
select a [fee mode](/launchpad/fees). The launch flow can include an optional
first purchase in the same transaction.

## BSCT, gas tokens, and testnet assets

[BSCT](/token) is Biscotti's protocol reward token. It is separate from USDC,
which pays for gas, and from the individual tokens created through the
launchpad. The current BSCT deployment is on Arc Testnet; its mainnet rollout
is tracked on the [Roadmap](/roadmap).

Arc Testnet uses chain ID `5042002`. Its test tokens and contract deployments
are separate from mainnet. Use the [testnet quick start](/quick-start) to
experiment with swaps and rewards.

## Arc token and airdrop questions

Finding a token on Arc, using a faucet, or holding BSCT does not establish
eligibility for an Arc airdrop. Each reward program needs its own published
rules. See [Arc airdrop questions](/arc-airdrop) for official information
sources and the rewards documented by Biscotti.
