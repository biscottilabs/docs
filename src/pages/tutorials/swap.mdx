# How to Swap on Arc [Four steps, about a minute]

**You need:** a wallet on Arc Mainnet with USDC for gas.
[Set one up](/arc-mainnet).

::::steps

### Open Swap and connect

Go to [biscotti.finance/swap](https://biscotti.finance/swap) and press
**Connect Wallet**. Check that the network reads **Arc Mainnet**.

![The Biscotti swap page on Arc Mainnet](/tutorials/swap-connect.webp)

### Pick your tokens

Choose what you pay (**From**) and what you get (**To**). Search by name or
paste a contract address.

![The token list: USDC, EURC, USYC and cirBTC](/tutorials/swap-select-token.webp)

### Enter an amount

The quote appears as you type: the rate, the minimum you'll receive and the
route.

![A USDC to EURC quote with rate, minimum received and route](/tutorials/swap-quote.webp)

### Swap

Press **Swap**, then **Confirm Swap** in your wallet. The first time you sell
a token, approve it once before the swap.

::::

## Good to know

- **Gas is paid in USDC.**
- **Check Min. received.** Big trades in small pools move the price.
- **Slippage** is Auto (0.5%). If a swap fails because the price moved, raise
  it slightly in ⚙ settings.
- **Launchpad tokens** are hidden by default. Turn on **Show launchpad tokens**
  in the token list, and check the address before you buy.
